# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals wanting a clean, minimal dashboard to track personal cash flow, net worth, and active loans.

## Product Purpose

FinTrack is a personal finance tracking application designed to aggregate accounts, transactions, and loans into a high-trust, centralized overview. It helps users manage their personal cash flow, monitor their net worth, and maintain clear awareness of their active financial liabilities.

## Brand Personality

Clean, technical, focused, classic, and luxurious. The interface should feel premium and professional, evoking a sense of calm, precision, and high-trust confidence.

## Anti-references

- Bloated enterprise dashboard layouts with excessive cards and unnecessary borders.
- Generic SaaS clichés like neon gradient titles, over-rounded cards (32px+), and low-contrast grey text.
- Cluttered grids that overstimulate the user.

## Design Principles

1. **Technical Luxury**: High-end details like crisp typographic alignment, balanced white space, and a curated dark palette. Quality is expressed through restraint.
2. **Clear Data Hierarchy**: Prioritize vital metrics (net worth, cash flow) clearly using size, weight, and layout, rather than busy graphs or decorative dividers.
3. **Calm and Confidence**: Use a dark, stable surface with subtle, purposeful accent states to make financial management feel reassuring rather than stressful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA color contrast compliance (at least 4.5:1 for body and placeholder text).
- Responsive layouts that adapt smoothly to different viewports.
- Respect system settings for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
